## POVERTY

Yes sirree I grew up poor. Not like in the way like my momma would need to scrounge together money to buy food or some shit, but like in the way I felt I was not in the "middle class" as the Simpsons would tell me. I grew up in an environment where I would not feel the LOVE between to people who raised me.

The thing is like wheneever like I write out this kinda shit it's like "so what, at least your mom/dad/guardiands didn't beat you our sexually abuse you" and I'm like "fuck, am I in the suffering olympics or some shit, I didn't know the bar was this high!"

Anyway the whole deal was that I was thinking about making new friends and I was like literally 13 beers in (I'm not kidding dude, I'm 105 kgs in and I can **drink** beer OK) in and I suddenly remembered about my favorite album of all time. It wasn't Jimi Hendrix or some shit, it was Emerson, Lake and Palmer's Tarkus.

The whole thing why it made me emotional was that the last time I bonded with this guy in high school was that we both liked ELP, and he was **really good** on the piano. And when we graduated from high school, he invited me over to his apartment in the same university town we were in. And boy howdy, I was poor, he was not. I shoulda known he was in a good family when he showed me his clavecin with black-and-white-keys and he had a house in Viimsi, a house one of the fanciest neighborhoods in Tallinn, with a bernhardin dog like in Beethoven.

I was envious all right! God damn, I was envious! I never spoke to him again after tha day when he invited me over to his apartment in Tartu, which was fucking sick. It was a really nice apartment dude.

I'm really sad about all these friends I had in high school, and some people are now dead (they straight up fucking killed themselves, ngl big sad), and some people I never talked to ever since I started university. Now it's been like what 15 years since I graduated? I still end up thinking about them sometimes - if only we could see eachother now.

I guess that's what nostalgia, the **pain** of the past and what **could've** been. I mean it's not a bad feeling, our brain is just trying to tell us things of what could have been.

Anyway I tried texting him through Facebook and then I realized FUCK some dudes never change. I tell them a joke, they'll be like "are you telling me a joke" and I'm like YES, motherfucker, I'm just kidding around!! God damn, dude! 

I don't know dude, where was I going with this thing. Uhhh, like your brain can tell you all these stories, but then you realize they are not true at all. Like I had some friends who killed themselves, but not all of them killed themselves at real (like the piano guy). So like, it is what it is, and enjoy what you can. IDK man that's the whole story LOL
