## IF THE MANUAL SUX, YOUR PROJECT'S DEAD, EXAMPLES.

I installed Melodee today, and IMO I think I'm gonna prefer this more.
Sure, Melodee only has like N amount of engines, but [Melodee's manual actually feels like a manual.](https://keremimo.github.io/melodee/webapp/manual/)

IDK brah I'm feeling it big time that when you need to judge if
the vibe coded slop is actually good or not,
all you need to do is read the manual.
If it's word salad, then that's a huge red flag.
[See: Sloop's manual.Play it live: hold a button, touch a key](https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1#play-it-live-hold-a-button-touch-a-key)

>Every function button is a layer: hold it and the 16 white keys and the four knobs change job, and the screen shows how.
>Tap it and its pages open.
>Hold a layer button and tap HOME to lock it open, both hands free.

WHAT THE FUCK IS THIS PARAGRAPH EVEN SUPPOSED TO BE?
How do the header and the paragraph even going together?

I really feel that since people don't read books anymore,
