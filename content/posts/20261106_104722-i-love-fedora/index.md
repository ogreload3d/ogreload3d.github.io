## I love Fedora

Here in my garage, just typing out on my new Fedora setup here.
It's fun to write up here in the Azabu-Juban Hills.
But you know what I like more than an efficient OS? KNOWLEDGE!
In fact I'm proud of that Fedora is using just a fraction of the memory on the SSD
and RAM that Windows would need to run a Start bar!
It's like billionaire Warren Buffet says,
"the more you learn about Microsoft,
you'll never stop wanting to beat B. Gates to death with your bare hands."

But in all seriousness, I'm a Fedora fanboy now.
God damn, I can't believe how inconvenient Windows had become,
and how Fedora is leaps and bounds ahead Ubuntu.
The progress that has been made is astonishing.

Anyway, I will admit: the setup part is HARD,
because you gotta start thinking like a computer user,
rather than a consumer.
You gotta think what is it you want to do with your computer,
and then let it do its thing.
Luckily enough the gap between MacOS and Fedora
isn't that big, so if you are already a Mac user, I think you'll love Fedora.
But if you are using Windows, and have never anything but Windows,
then you might have a hard time at first.
Definitely not a "afternoon project", more like a "weekend project" lol.

Of course if you are a "power user" who needs to use idk Adobe
and other proprietary software, tough shit.
But if you are just a guy who needs a text editor,
maybe Blender and Godot, Discord etc...
Not a big deal at all indeed.

Anyway that was the quick update.
Just wanted to share few of my screenshots here lol.
