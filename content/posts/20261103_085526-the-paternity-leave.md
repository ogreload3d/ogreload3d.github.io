## Daddy's daycare

Well here I am at my paternity leave. The first day I was with my baby I said to myself: "I can't freaking WAIT to just waste this entire month doing nothing." Well guess what big dog, I'm Type A as heck dude. If Alec Baldwin was there and telling me "Always Be Closing" I'd be closing a drawer, a door, a sale, anything dude. And now I'm here, I got 4 entire weeks of Paternity Leave, and between getting puked and shat on, I am thinking "How can I possibly monetize this?" Well, the good news is that I'm not planning to get rich quick, but at least set up the seeds for my future success. 

I've been really into this entire BBS aesthetic these days. I know like I used to be into darts big time last month and now I don't play darts, but not because I lost interest in it, but because of my new life situation. The thing is that your hobbies etc. need to fit your LIFE, not your life around your hobbies IMO. So I've been thinking OK how can I have a hobby that'll be something fun, and what I can start doing and stop doing any time I have the time.

For example, I used to play a lot of video games. But Video Games suck you in, yknow, and when I am on a roll, you can't even "pause the game" as you used to back in the day. So video games are kinda out of the picture, since I can't really play and feed the kid the same time.

Writing is one of the hobbies that I have for real done ever since I was in high school. Like for real, this is the only hobby that I've stuck with. I guess that's because you don't need much to write: either you use a computer, or you'll use a pen and paper. I have like 15 years worth of diaries with my chicken scratch handwriting and all the tickets etc. sandwiched between the pages. [It's called Ephemera, you aren't really supposed to keep these posters, tickets etc. but they hold a very valuable information about the places you've been to and what those places provided. ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ephemera) Also the design sensibilities of the time etc. You can tell when a thing was roughly printed just by the paper and typography used!

I also like making music but y'know I'm living in a quiet house, and times when I wanna make music is when my wife wants to be quiet and do her things LOL. So I'm like ok music making is kinda in the picture, but maybe making it is a bit "not everywhere, not anytime" kind of thing.

So what's the point of the story? The thing is that writing for me is the only hobby I can do. There's not much difference between writing code and writing these texts TBF. I mean, obviously I'm exaggerating here, but once you hit that "flow state" there's really not much difference, since you're just moving and/or writing text.

What's the future of this blog then? I'll of course write down my stuff in the blog, but the point is that my BBS will be another way to interact with my stuff. And you guys know how much I LOVE when I have my computers do all my work for me, so I write into this place once, and all my BBS etc. will go and get my stuff for me haha.
